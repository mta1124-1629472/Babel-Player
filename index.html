Local-first AI video dubbing

Turn video dialogue into editable dubbed speech without hiding the pipeline.

Babel Player is a desktop workstation for loading local media, generating timed transcripts, translating dialogue, assigning voices, previewing dubbed speech in context, and refining the result before export.

The workflow

Built around staged refinement, not one-shot magic.

Dubbing quality comes from iteration. Babel Player keeps each stage visible so you can fix the source of a problem instead of rerunning a black box.

01

Ingest

Load local media, extract audio, and prepare the project workspace.

02

Transcript

Generate timed dialogue segments that can be edited before downstream stages.

03

Translate

Create target-language dialogue while preserving segment timing and speaker context.

04

Assign voices

Map speakers to voices, review language/provider choices, and keep settings explicit.

05

Generate TTS

Render dubbed speech per segment so individual fixes do not require a full rerun.

06

Preview & refine

Hear dubbed speech against the original video, then adjust text, timing, voice, or mix.

Why Babel Player

A dubbing workstation should tell the truth about its state.

The goal is not to dazzle you with a loading spinner. It is to preserve your work, show which stage produced which artifacts, and make local AI workloads less fiddly.

Local media first

Projects begin with files on your machine, not forced uploads.

Resumable work

Generated artifacts are saved so reopening a project does not mean recomputing everything.

Clear provider routing

CPU, GPU, and optional provider paths should be visible instead of guessed after the fact.

Segment-level iteration

Fix one line, regenerate one line, preview one range. The pipeline should not become a slot machine.

Project status

Open-source and still evolving.

Babel Player is under active development. Expect rough edges, but the product direction is practical: reliable desktop workflow first, flashy automation second.

Core direction

Desktop-first dubbing pipeline with local workflow, staged AI processing, preview, and refinement.

Important caveat

Model and voice licenses vary. Commercial use depends on the specific providers, binaries, and model weights you configure.
